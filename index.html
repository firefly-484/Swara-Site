About Me

Hello! My name is Swara Warke. I am a 9th grade student passionate about mathematics, music, and problem solving, and I enjoy balancing critical thinking with creativity. Math is a major passion of mine. I love the process of thinking through challenging problems and exploring different solutions. I like challenging myself by working through problems and participating in competitions such as MathCounts and the AMC. I am also deeply involved in the Arts. I have been playing piano for 8 years and sing in advanced choir ensembles, including being selected for All-State Chorus for 2 consecutive years, where I collaborated with high-performing musicians from across the state. I served as Chorus President in SFMS in the 2025-2026 school year, helping lead and support my peers. I also received the 2025 Hughston Foundation Art Gala Mental Health Award for my creative work. In addition, I founded a coding club at my middle school using CodeHS to introduce students to programming. I have experience with Python and Java and enjoy exploring technology as a tool for problem-solving. In my free time, I enjoy playing the piano or reading an interesting book!
